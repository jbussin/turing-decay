Findings from the pilot
−5.3

points per generator-year

Within the GAN lineage, faces from generators released a year later were detected about 5 points less often (p = .006).

+0.2

points per calendar year

Against a fixed generator (StyleGAN2), five years of studies show no gain. Background exposure did not help (p = .82).

95%

for frequent LLM users

Novices spotted GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 and o1-pro text 52% of the time. People who use ChatGPT for writing spotted it 95% of the time.

Audio and video: what the meta-analysis found

We tested the preregistered predictions on 125 participant groups from 58 studies of AI-generated audio and video, screened from 7,609 records. A negative slope means people do worse against newer generators.

+17

points above chance

People still tell real from AI-generated audio and video better than a coin flip, for audio and video alike.

−0.23

d′ per generator-year, audio

Listeners' sensitivity falls with each year of newer voice generator (95% CI −0.43 to −0.04). Raw accuracy points the same way but is not significant.

1.3 yr

half-life, text-to-video

Video shows no decline overall, but within text-to-video generators people's margin above chance halves in about 1.3 years.

Who is watching matters too: people caught clones of their own or a friend's voice 86–90% of the time, while strangers were at chance on the same clones. No directional prediction survives the registered correction for multiple tests, and 54 included studies report no usable numbers.

The model

The human advantage over chance, A, is set by three factors. Within an architecture family f it decays as generator capability C rises. Deployment effort D pushes it down, below zero if the prompting is good enough. Hands-on expertise E is the only factor that pushes back.

A = A0,f · e−λf(C − C0,f) − δ·D + γ·E

Three factors in text

1 · Generator capability

Same release date, bigger model, lower accuracy

GPT-3 news articles, eight sizes released together (Brown et al., 2020). Accuracy falls 6.5 points per tenfold increase in parameters (r = −0.87), reaching chance at about 175B. Time is held constant, so capability is doing the work.

2 · Deployment effort

How the model is prompted can outweigh which model it is

Three-party Turing test, 5-minute chats (Jones & Bergen, 2025). Accuracy is the share of games where the interrogator picked the real human. Below 50%, the AI was picked as human more often than the human was.

3 · Observer expertise

Heavy users catch what novices miss

Articles from GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet and o1-pro (Russell et al., 2025). Based on 4 novices and 5 experts, so treat the size of the gap as provisional.

Preregistered predictions

Registered on 27 September 2026 (release v0.1.0), before any audio or video accuracy value was coded. Each applies to audio and video separately and names the result that would refute it. The verdicts come from the meta-analysis above.

H1

Dots are estimates with 95% confidence intervals from the three-level meta-regression (125 groups, 58 studies). The shaded band is the region the preregistration predicted. Units are percentage points unless noted.

  1. Generator decay. Within a lineage, newer generators yield lower accuracy.Refuted if the release-date slope is zero or positive in both modalities.Not refuted · audio d′ declines, accuracy not significant; video flat
  2. Sawtooth reset. The first generator of a new architecture family is more detectable than the old lineage's trend predicts.Refuted if the reset term is zero or negative.Not supported · too few reset groups
  3. No background observer clock. For a fixed generator, untrained accuracy does not rise with the year a study was run.Refuted if that slope is reliably above +2 points per year.Inconclusive · intervals still include +2 points per year
  4. Expertise. Heavy users of voice-cloning or video-generation tools detect better than untrained participants.Refuted if the difference is zero or negative.Not testable · only 5 expert groups
  5. Deployment effort. Curated or post-processed stimuli (phone-codec audio, compressed social video) are harder to catch than raw outputs of the same generator.Refuted if the difference is zero or positive.Not supported · video leans the predicted way (p = .16)
  6. Modality ordering. Video decays more slowly than audio.Refuted if video's slope is steeper than audio's.Predicted direction · not significant

Point predictions, recorded for scoring: audio stays above chance for the newest generators studied (supported: +11.7 points at the newest generator), and video decays at under half the per-year rate of GAN faces (supported: −0.1 points per year).

Code & data

Everything behind the figures is in this repository. The analysis is a random-effects meta-regression (DerSimonian-Laird, Knapp-Hartung) in plain NumPy and SciPy.
